# Scientific claims and validation layers

Keep these layers separate: reduced simulation, model-derived candidate
reconstruction, external atomistic validation, synthetic spectroscopy,
experimental measurement, statistical comparison, and conclusion. Agreement
at one layer supports progression to the next; it does not prove the next.

The current reduced torsion has no unique three-dimensional interpretation.
Idealized coordinates have no side chains, solvent, protonation assignment, or
energetic refinement. OpenMM settings and force fields are modeling choices.
Neither a reconstructed fold, short-lived stability, nor a spectral Fourier
maximum proves quantum-directed protein folding.
